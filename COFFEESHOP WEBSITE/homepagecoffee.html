Three things we won't compromise on

Everything else about running a café is negotiable. These aren't.

Traceable beans

Every lot is bought direct from three family farms we've visited ourselves — no anonymous blends, no mystery origin.

Roasted this week

We roast Tuesdays and Fridays only, in batches small enough that nothing waits more than five days before it's in your cup.

Dialed in daily

Our baristas re-calibrate every grinder each morning, because yesterday's settings are already wrong today.
